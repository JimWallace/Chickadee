### Added

- **Seasonal avatar rings.** Three new rings, one per Waterloo term: snowflake (Winter), blossom (Spring) and maple (Fall). A student can choose a seasonal ring only during its term, read from today's date on the Waterloo calendar, and keeps it after the term ends. Out of its term, the account page shows the ring disabled with its term in the name. See `docs/student-wardrobe.md`, "Seasonal rings".
