### Added

- **A graded toast in the notebook editor.** When an attempt is graded while the student is still in the notebook, the task headers, checks lines and outline update in place, and a toast under Submit gives the grade and how many public checks passed, with See results and Keep editing. At 100% the bird hops once under three stars. A worker-graded submission no longer leaves the notebook for the results page while the editor extension is loaded. See `docs/notebook-guide.md`, "When an attempt is graded".
