### Changed

- **The Activity tab reads as a day-by-day log.** Rows are grouped under Today, Yesterday and dated headings in the course timezone. Each row is one sentence (who did what to what), with a coloured tile for the kind of event, the category and time underneath, and an eye link when there is somewhere to go.
- **The person filter is a select of course staff.** It replaces the free-text box and its Filter and Clear buttons, applies on change, and keeps the same `actor` query value.
