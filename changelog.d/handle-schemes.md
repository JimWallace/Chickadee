### Changed

- **New class handles in three schemes.** A new handle comes from one of three schemes, each with a chance of 1 in 3: a positive disposition and a scientist ("Curious Noether", "Bold Wang Zhenyi"), a science noun and an agent ("Photon Navigator"), or a compound word ("Ionspark"). The scientist list has 436 people, 43% women and 34% from Europe. The draw prefers a scientist that nobody in the course uses yet. A stored handle does not change: the shape check now accepts one to three words, with hyphens, internal capitals and non-ASCII letters, and every handle that was valid before stays valid. The review tool reads all three schemes, checks compound words for unwanted substrings, and reports the balance targets and each living person for a person to check. See `docs/student-avatars.md` §3.
