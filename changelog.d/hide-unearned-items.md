### Changed

- **The account page no longer lists items a student has not earned.** Faces and rings that a student earns are surprises: they are not shown until they are earned, so a list of items not earned yet cannot read as a list of failures (#2615). A closed "How you earned these" section now says how each earned item was earned, in the past tense. A seasonal ring out of its term still shows its "opens in" line, because every student gets it.
