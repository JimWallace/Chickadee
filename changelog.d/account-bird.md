### Changed

- **Account page: "Your bird".** The Species picker is gone: the species is drawn, not chosen. A student may change the colour (a colourway of their own species, plus the backdrop) and the ring. `AvatarCustomization` refuses a colourway of another species, so a hand-made request cannot change the species either. The bird moves out of Account info into its own card at the podium size. Rings that are not open yet are listed under "Not open yet" and are no longer disabled inputs. Save stays disabled until a choice changes, and "Undo changes" returns to the saved choices.
