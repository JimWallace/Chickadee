### Added

- **A loading panel over the notebook editor.** While the editor starts, a panel shows the viewer's bird pecking at seeds and three steps: load the editor, download and start the language, open the notebook. The steps follow the kernel phases that the editor already reports, and the panel fades out when the kernel is idle. A kernel chip in the toolbar shows "starting" and then "ready". The slow-boot notice, the low-memory notice and the failure panel work as before; the failure panel replaces the loading panel.
