### Changed

- **The LEARN page states the connection as facts.** A Connected or Paused pill sits in the title bar, and a facts card shows who the course pushes as, the org unit and the last sync, with Test connection and a ⋯ menu for Change org unit. Grade items are rows with a mapped-state dot, a synced and failed count, and the failure text, and the unreachable-student roster shows each student's avatar.
- **The per-instructor LEARN controls and the grades CSV link are hidden when the deployment has a service account.** This covers the connect form, "Use my account", "Disconnect my account" and "Export Grades CSV". They are hidden, not removed: the routes stay, and the controls come back on a deployment with no service account.
