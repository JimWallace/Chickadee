### Added

- **Earn items for your bird.** A student's own graded work now unlocks wardrobe items: the chirp face for a first graded submission, the sly face when every test passes on a first attempt, and the two-tone ring when every test passes on three assignments. The account page grants them, never the grading path, and an unlock is kept after a regrade. Locked items say how to earn them, and the data export lists what was earned (#2592).
