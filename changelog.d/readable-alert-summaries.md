### Changed

- **Health alerts say what is wrong, what it is about, and where to look.** "Runner offline" names the runners last seen and gives the time in minutes. "Jobs no runner can grade" and "BrightSpace grade sync failing" name the assignments. "Editor kernel unrecoverable", "System-level failure rate spike", "Database unreachable" and "Auto-deploy not healthy" say what the failure means and what to check. A list of names stops at five, and the full list stays in the alert's details.
