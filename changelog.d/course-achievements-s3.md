### Added

- **Course achievements: the model and the evaluator (S3).** A course can now
  hold achievements that count across its assignments: assignments graded, at
  a grade, at full marks, early, comebacks, assignment badges earned, and
  concepts mastered. Each one counts the assignments students can see, in the
  whole course, one course section, one concept or a chosen list. A student's
  own result page and account page store the ones they earn in the award
  ledger. The editing tab and the MCP tools come in the next slices
  (docs/achievement-rewards.md).
